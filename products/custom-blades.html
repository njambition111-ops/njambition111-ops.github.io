Your Design, Our Precision

Not every production line uses standard blades. We specialize in reverse-engineering your existing blades or fabricating entirely new designs from your CAD files, technical drawings, or physical samples. Same CH5/CH8 steel grades and vacuum hardening process — for any machine brand.
