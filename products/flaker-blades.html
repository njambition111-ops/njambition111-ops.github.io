Flaker Blades

High-precision flaking knives engineered for surface and core layer production across all major panel board formats.

Overview

Our flaker blades deliver uniform flake thickness, reduced energy consumption, and longer production runs between blade changes. Manufactured to exacting tolerances with proprietary edge geometry, our blades are trusted by particle board, MDF, and OSB manufacturers globally.
