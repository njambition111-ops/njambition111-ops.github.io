Chipper Knives

Precision-engineered chipper blades for every major wood chipping machine type in the panel board industry.

Overview

Our chipper knives are manufactured from premium high-speed steel (HSS) and tungsten carbide materials, heat-treated and precision-ground to deliver consistent chip geometry, reduced machine downtime, and extended service life. We supply blades for ring chippers, drum chippers, and disc chippers used in MDF, HDF, particle board, and OSB production.
