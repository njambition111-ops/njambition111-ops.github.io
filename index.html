Industries We Serve

Powering Panel Production Globally

Our knives are at work in production lines across every major wood-based panel segment.

MDF Production

Medium-density fiberboard lines require consistent chip geometry. Our chipper knives deliver uniform raw material for high-quality MDF output.

HDF / Laminate Flooring

High-density fiberboard demands superior wear resistance. Tungsten carbide-edged blades for extended production runs.

Particle Board

Surface and core layer flaking blades optimized for particle board production. Precise flake thickness control.

OSB (Oriented Strand Board)

Heavy-duty stranding knives for OSB production. Engineered for high-impact durability in demanding operations.
