Klöckner Chipper Series: Complete Blade Compatibility Guide

Home › Blog › Klöckner Blades

Published: June 19, 2026 | By PanelKnife

Klöckner is one of the most widely deployed chipper brands in the global wood panel industry, with machines operating in OSB, MDF, and particleboard plants across six continents. If you run a Klöckner chipper, understanding blade compatibility — dimensions, steel grades, and mounting configurations — is essential to keeping your operation running efficiently. This guide covers the major Klöckner chipper series and the blades that fit them.

Major Klöckner Chipper Models

Klöckner's drum chipper lineup spans from compact models designed for sawmill residue processing to heavy-duty units that handle full-tree logs. The most common series in production today include the Klöckner HRL series (horizontal feed, ideal for slab wood and edgings), the Klöckner VTH series (vertical feed, preferred for high-volume panel plants), and the Klöckner UHM series (universal heavy-duty, built for logs up to 800mm diameter). Each series uses a specific blade geometry optimized for its cutting geometry and material flow characteristics.

Standard Blade Dimensions

Klöckner chippers use three primary blade sizes, and knowing your exact dimensions is non-negotiable for proper fitment:

  • 530 x 120 x 20 mm — Used on HRL-400 and VTH-400 series machines. These are the smallest Klöckner blades, typically running in sets of 8 to 12 knives per rotor. Counter-knife (bed knife) dimensions are 530 x 80 x 20 mm.
  • 630 x 210 x 20 mm — Used on HRL-600, VTH-600, and UHM-600 series. These blades run in sets of 10 to 16. The 210mm width provides extended wear life between sharpenings, typically 8–12 production hours in normal chip production.
  • 830 x 210 x 20 mm — Used on UHM-800 and large-frame VTH-800 machines. These heavy blades weigh approximately 27 kg each and run in sets of 12 to 20. Mounting bolt patterns are M20 with 200mm center-to-center spacing.

Additional sizes exist for specialty applications: 480 x 120 x 20 mm for the compact HRL-300 series, and 930 x 230 x 25 mm for the UHM-1000 mega-chipper. Always verify bolt hole diameter (typically 20.5mm for M20 bolts) and center distances before ordering replacements.

Steel Grades for Klöckner Blades

Klöckner OEM blades are manufactured from high-speed steel, most commonly the DIN 1.3343 grade (equivalent to AISI M2). This grade offers an excellent balance of toughness and wear resistance at 60–64 HRC. For applications processing frozen wood or material with high silica content, some operators upgrade to DIN 1.3207 (AISI M42) which contains 8% cobalt for enhanced hot hardness. The base material is typically supplied with mill test certificates per EN 10204 3.1, ensuring full traceability from melt to finished blade. Tungsten carbide-tipped blades are also available for extreme-wear applications, though these require diamond grinding for resharpening.

Aftermarket Compatibility

Many plant managers choose aftermarket blades to reduce operating costs while maintaining performance. The key to successful aftermarket sourcing is dimensional precision: blade length tolerance should be ±0.3mm, thickness tolerance ±0.05mm, and cutting edge straightness within 0.1mm over the full blade length. Mounting hole positions must match the original pattern exactly — even 0.5mm deviation can cause uneven clamping and blade vibration.
