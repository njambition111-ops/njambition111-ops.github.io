Who We Are

Nanjing Ambixin Machine Tool Co., LTD is a China-based manufacturer specializing in the research, development, and production of industrial cutting knives for the wood-based panel industry. Operating under the brand PanelKnife, we produce high-performance chipper knives, flaker blades, and custom-engineered cutting solutions for MDF, HDF, particle board, and OSB production lines.

With over 20 years of manufacturing experience, our facility in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province combines CNC machining, precision heat treatment, and rigorous quality inspection to produce blades that meet the exacting standards of panel producers in more than 50 countries. From standard specifications to fully custom OEM designs, every PanelKnife blade is engineered for durability, cutting precision, and maximum production uptime.

Our Values

From our factory in Nanjing to your production line — we handle all export documentation, logistics, and after-sales support. Consistent quality, on-time delivery, competitive pricing.
