About PanelKnife

Precision engineering meets industrial reliability. We've been manufacturing cutting solutions for the global panel industry for over two decades.

Who We Are

PanelKnife is a specialized manufacturer of industrial cutting tools for the wood-based panel industry. Our core products — chipper knives and flaker blades — are used in MDF, HDF, particle board, and OSB production lines across more than 50 countries.

From our manufacturing facility, we combine advanced CNC machining, precision heat treatment, and rigorous quality control to produce blades that meet the exacting standards of global panel producers. Every blade is inspected for dimensional accuracy, hardness, and edge quality before shipment.

Our Values

Precision First

Tolerances measured in microns. Every blade meets or exceeds OEM specifications.

Customer Partnership

We work closely with production managers to optimize blade life and reduce total cost of ownership.

Continuous Improvement

Investing in new materials, coatings, and manufacturing processes to stay at the forefront of cutting technology.

Want to Work With Us?
